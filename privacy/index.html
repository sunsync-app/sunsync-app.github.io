Privacy Policy for Sunsync

Effective Date: April, 18, 2025

Sunsync respects your privacy. This app is designed to provide times and notifications for daily solar events. We are committed to keeping your data safe — and in most cases, completely out of our hands.

What We Collect

Sunsync does not collect or store any personal data on our servers or externally from your device.

The app uses the following third-party services to provide functionality and improve performance:

  • Google Analytics – to understand general usage trends (not tied to individual users)
  • Google Crashlytics – to identify and fix bugs and crashes
  • Google Places API & Geocoding API – to get place names and information
  • TimeAPI.io – to get timezone data from location information

Refer to the privacy policy of the Google services to learn more.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information — because we don’t collect any. All external services mentioned above operate under their own privacy policies.

Children’s Privacy

Sunsync is intended for users of all ages.

Your Control

Since we do not collect or store user data, there is no account or personal information to delete. The data stays on your device, and you’re always in control.

Changes to This Policy

If there are any changes to this policy, we’ll update this page and reflect the new effective date at the top.
